# Testimonial requests — ready to personalize and send

Per your call on PLAN.md item 9: send these to the supervisors most likely to
reply, in this order. Two replies are enough; three is the cap. Each email is
short on purpose — the easier the ask, the faster the yes.

**What to fill in:** the `[brackets]`, plus one sentence of shared context only
you know (a specific bug you fixed, a meeting where they complimented the work).
That detail is what turns a generic quote into a specific one.

**When quotes come back,** paste them into the thread and item 9 gets built —
stacked reveal layout on /about, linked from home, permission already covered.

## Ask etiquette (why these emails are shaped this way)

- **Specific over general:** each email names the project, so the reply quotes
  real work instead of producing "Omar was great, highly recommend."
- **The example quote:** showing what a good quote looks like doubles the
  chance of getting a usable one in the first reply.
- **Name-and-role is the ask up front:** PLAN.md item 9's acceptance requires
  named social proof; negotiating attribution after the fact is awkward.
- **Cheap no:** every email offers an easy decline — preserves the
  relationship, which matters more than any single quote.
