## Problem

I wanted a compiler frontend I understood end-to-end: characters in, parse trees out, with recovery when the source is wrong.

## Approach

I built the frontend from scratch in C/C++. A hand-coded lexer uses custom regular expressions and Deterministic Finite Automata for tokenization and error recovery. A formal Context-Free Grammar covers functions, control-flow loops, conditionals, and multivariable arithmetic. A recursive-descent parser performs strict syntax analysis and generates visual parse trees.

## Outcome

The compiler accepts a small language through lexing and parsing with explicit error paths — automata and grammar trade-offs made concrete rather than theoretical.

## Stack

C, C++, regular expressions, DFAs, CFG, recursive-descent parsing.
