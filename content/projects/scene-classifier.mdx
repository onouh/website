## Problem

Scene labels on messy images need more than a stock classifier: preprocessing, architecture choice, and honest validation.

## Approach

I built an end-to-end pipeline in Python: distortion-aware preprocessing, categorical encoding, and feature engineering, then fine-tuned convolutional and transformer models (including ConvNeXt-class and Swin-style architectures) in PyTorch with scikit-learn in the evaluation loop.

## Outcome

The pipeline reached **60.3% validation accuracy** on the target structured dataset — a measured baseline, not a leaderboard claim.

## Stack

Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, transfer learning, CNNs, transformers.
