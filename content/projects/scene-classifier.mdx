## Problem

17-way indoor scene-style classification on messy competition imagery: class imbalance, small effective batches, and backbones that disagree. A single stock fine-tune plateaus fast — the gains live in the training loop, the loss function, and the ensemble.

## Approach

I built a PyTorch Lightning pipeline around a swappable backbone registry — ResNet-50, EfficientNet-B4, five ConvNeXt sizes, and Swin Tiny/Small — with a custom classification head (LayerNorm → GELU MLP) that adapts to each backbone's feature geometry: adaptive pooling for CNN maps, mean-over-tokens for sequence models like Swin.

Training is two-phase: the backbone starts frozen while only the head trains, then an `UnfreezeBackboneCallback` unfreezes it at a configured epoch for careful full fine-tuning with a 100× LR differential (backbone 1e-5, head 3e-4) under a warmup + cosine LambdaLR schedule. When a checkpoint lacks a matching head, a shape-filtered loader strips classifier tensors and loads only backbone weights that match by name and shape.

The custom `StyleAwareLoss` combines label-smoothed cross-entropy with an optional **Gram-matrix texture loss** — Gram matrices computed for both CNN `(B, C, H, W)` and token-sequence `(B, N, D)` feature layouts — pulling feature activations toward those of horizontally flipped views to encourage texture-consistent representations.

For the leaderboard, a weighted ensemble script loads the best checkpoints (Swin-Small 54.8%, ConvNeXt-Base 60.3% val accuracy) behind their correct head architectures, applies 7-view test-time augmentation per model per image, and blends weight-normalized probabilities. Everything is tracked in Weights & Biases with 16-mixed precision, gradient clipping, and early stopping.

## Outcome

ConvNeXt-Base reached **60.3% validation accuracy** on the 17-class task; the TTA ensemble combined the strongest checkpoints into the final submission — a measured, reproducible result, not a leaderboard claim.

## Stack

Python, PyTorch, PyTorch Lightning, torchvision v2, torchmetrics, Gram-matrix style loss, TTA ensembling, Weights & Biases.
