## Problem

A clothing factory needed one place to see materials, production stages, and fulfillment without spreadsheet chaos or silent data errors.

## Approach

I designed a multi-sheet relational tracker in Excel (with JavaScript where useful) for raw materials, production, and orders. Dynamic validation, INDEX/MATCH lookups, and conditional formatting catch bad entry. Pivot Tables and summary dashboards surface bottlenecks instead of burying them in rows.

## Outcome

Operations has real-time visibility into inventory levels and process stalls, with fewer manual-entry failures.

## Stack

Microsoft Excel, JavaScript, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables, data validation.
