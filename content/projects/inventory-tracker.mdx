## Problem

A clothing factory needed one place to track raw materials, production stages, and order fulfillment — where a manual-entry error could not silently corrupt stock records. Scattered spreadsheets bury both the data and the mistakes.

## Approach

I designed a multi-sheet relational tracker in Microsoft Excel (with JavaScript where it helped) covering three domains: materials, production, and orders. Data validation gates every input field, INDEX/MATCH lookups join records across sheets relationally, and conditional formatting flags inconsistencies visually. Automated Pivot Tables and summary dashboards give management a live view of operational bottlenecks and inventory levels instead of buried rows.

## Outcome

One workbook as the factory's single source of truth: three tracked domains, entry errors caught at the gate by validation and lookups, and real-time dashboards that surface stalls as they happen. Manual-entry errors — the failure mode that makes spreadsheets untrustworthy — are eliminated at input, not discovered at stocktake.

## Stack

Microsoft Excel, JavaScript, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables, data validation.
